The Bitter Lesson - Rich Sutton

22/11/2026

I'm not entirely sure what to write about this essay, but you should definitely read it. AI can make everyone sad and disgruntled, even AI researchers.

The unfortunate reality differs between groups; jobs are taken, art is stolen, and researchers, who are supposed to advance things with their smart ideas, can have their ideas replaced by results obtained by huge amounts of compute and data. The same (perhaps slightly tweaked at times) method is applied to every problem, search and learn.

In a way, it is only as bitter as the extent to which people pride themselves on their research or genius algorithms or whatnot, but to have your area of expertise trumped by a single methodology would still hurt. However the importance of what they are trying to achieve versus how are they trying to achieve it is still important.

So yes, it is a rude awakening in a way that using search and learn will outperform smart human ideas about certain things in the long run (with enough compute), but I guess that is/could be the price of progress? People have been replaced by machines many times before and this likely won't be the last time, but it is still sad to love what you do and replaced by a computer.
